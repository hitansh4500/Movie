How to Add CoCoFlix to Your Home Screen

Install CoCoFlix as a web app on any device with 1 click — no app download required

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome / Brave)

Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right and tap Install App or Add to Home screen.

Mac & Windows PC

In Chrome or Edge, click the Install icon in the address bar to install CoCoFlix as a standalone desktop app.

Smart TV & FireStick
